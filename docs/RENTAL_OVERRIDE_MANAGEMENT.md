# Assignment rental overrides

Open **Tenant Management → tenant → Rental Information**, select the assignment's reference month, and use **Manual override request → Continue** to open the proposal. Automatic rent remains the default and uses the assignment's frozen initial assessment. An override belongs to one eligible assignment and never carries forward to its successor.

1. Choose **Rate per square meter** to multiply the proposed rate by the frozen assessed area, or **Direct daily amount** to enter the daily rent itself. The preview compares proposed and automatic rent, including the base monthly rent, CUSA and total for the selected month.
2. Choose prospective full months within the assignment term. Enter the reason and any supporting reference. Save a draft or submit for review. Drafts and pending requests have no effect on applicable rent.
3. A different authorized officer reviews the pending request in **Override history**. The exact displayed policy must be approved and cover the entire requested period. Out-of-range rent requires the explicit policy grant, policy authority reference and supporting request reference. Zero rent also requires the explicit exemption grant.
4. Approve or reject the proposal. Confirm applicable rent in the tenant record or market map for the covered month. The **Rental Override Report** distinguishes the requested terms from the amount applicable on the selected date. Assessments and financial records retain their saved calculation basis.
5. An authorized officer can use **Return to automatic** from a prospective full-month boundary, with a reason. Expiry or revocation restores the frozen automatic basis when the manual terms no longer apply. The system blocks changes that would conflict with frozen bills or contract obligations.

The calculator uses exact decimal arithmetic. Monthly rent is the applicable daily rent multiplied by the assessed billable days. The original policy basis supplies CUSA and applicable advance and deposit rules. Billing drafts still need explicit posting. A new lease requires consistent terms across the term. Generation and issuance also require its two-decimal rate and area to reproduce the applicable daily amount, including for direct daily overrides.

Access follows the established permission checks for viewing, requesting, approval, rejection, revocation, history and reporting. Operators should verify actual role grants after the additive RoleSeeder and obtain the LGU's policy approval before approving an operational override. The fictional manual exercise confers no operational authority.
